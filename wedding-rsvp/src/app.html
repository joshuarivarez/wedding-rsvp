OUR STORY

Some things are
meant to be.

What started as a few chance encounters through mutual friends turned into a friendship built through simple chats and constant check-ins. As we got to know each other better, friendship blossomed into love. After a few dates, we officially became a couple on February 8, 2020.

On February 7, 2026, the proposal happened, making our journey even more special. Now, we are excited to begin our next chapter as we prepare to say “I do” on February 8, 2027—exactly seven years after our love story began.

From strangers, to friends, to forever. ♥
