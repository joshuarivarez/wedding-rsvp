What should I wear?+

Garden semi-formal. Think light fabrics, earthy tones, and shoes that are happy on a garden path. We encourage our guests to dress within our wedding color palette.

We kindly ask our guests to refrain from wearing white or champagne, as these colors are reserved for the bride.

Can I bring a plus-one?+

Please follow the guest list provided on your invitation. We’re keeping our celebration intimate and kindly ask that only the named guests attend.

A note on gifts+

Your presence is our favorite present, and sharing this special day with you is more than enough. Should you wish to bless us with a gift, we would warmly appreciate a monetary gift in place of a physical one as we begin this new chapter together.

Getting to the venue+

Join us at 10 22 Lipa, Alaminos Rd., Lipa City, Batangas. Our wedding ceremony begins at 4:00 PM. We would greatly appreciate your arrival by 3:30 PM, giving you plenty of time to settle in and get comfortable before the ceremony begins. View on Google Maps ↗
